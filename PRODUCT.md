# Product

## Register

product

## Users

UCF SASE members use the site to discover events, manage their account, and complete club workflows. Student officers use authenticated admin tools to run events, review participation, and reconcile membership dues quickly and accurately, often while balancing other club responsibilities.

## Product Purpose

The site is the operational home for the UCF Society of Asian Scientists and Engineers chapter. It should make joining, participating, and administering the club straightforward. Success means members understand what to do next, while officers can complete sensitive tasks with confidence and a clear audit trail.

## Brand Personality

Bold, optimistic, and community-centered. The experience should feel technical and future-facing without becoming corporate, sterile, or generic. Administrative workflows should stay calmer than public brand moments while still feeling unmistakably UCF SASE.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, excessive card grids, decorative glass effects, stock illustration, anonymous corporate styling, and interfaces that hide important state behind color alone. Do not expose internal payment identifiers, matching logic, or raw provider data to members.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next action unmistakable.
2. Keep sensitive decisions explicit, reversible, and easy to audit.
3. Let community and events carry the personality; let tools stay focused.
4. Reuse familiar controls and the established UCF SASE visual language.
5. Explain errors with a direct recovery path.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast and interaction expectations. Preserve keyboard-visible focus, semantic structure, 44px touch targets, reduced-motion support, and text or icon labels for every status. Responsive layouts must remain usable at mobile widths without horizontal page overflow.
